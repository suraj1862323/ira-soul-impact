YOUR MOMENT, SHARED

Every occasion can
mean a little more.

There's no required event, minimum sentiment or perfect reason to give. Choose the moment that matters to you, or choose no occasion at all.

Tell us your occasion ↗
01

Birthdays

Celebrate another year by sharing meals, learning supplies, essentials or a thoughtful activity. You can make it a solo gesture, family tradition or a tribute from friends.

02

Anniversaries & weddings

Mark a partnership or wedding by turning part of the celebration into a kindness activity. A shared contribution can reflect the values you want your relationship to carry forward.

03

New beginnings & achievements

Celebrate a new job, promotion, graduation, exam success, business milestone or personal achievement with an act that shares your excitement beyond yourself.

04

Festivals & family celebrations

Make festive joy more inclusive with food, essentials, seasonal care, environment activities or another thoughtful plan. Families can make it a repeatable tradition.

05

New baby & housewarming

Celebrate a new arrival or a new home by sharing practical care—such as essentials or meals—with someone else. You can keep the activity simple and meaningful.

06

In memory / remembrance

Honor someone who mattered to you through a gentle act of care. You can share a few words about their memory if you'd like, but we won't require personal details.

07

Business opening & milestones

Celebrate launching a shop, office, venture or milestone with a community activity. A business can share a moment of gratitude while creating a positive local gesture.

08

Just because

No birthday, calendar or milestone needed. Sometimes the reason is simply that you want to help today—and that's a reason enough to explore.

Not sure what to choose?

Tell us the occasion and budget. We can suggest activity ideas and explain what each option could include before you decide.
