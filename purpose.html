OUR BELIEF

Small thoughtful acts
can make moments
feel bigger.

People often want to help but may not know what to organize, how to source things, or how to follow through. We are building a service that makes occasion-based kindness approachable: choose a moment, select an activity, set a budget, and work with us to coordinate it.

Our purpose isn't to claim we solve every social challenge. It's to help individuals and families take a practical step, thoughtfully and transparently—and to make sharing part of how they celebrate.

OUR PRINCIPLES

How we want to show up.

Respect & dignity ↗

Activities should respect people receiving support. We avoid treating anyone as a prop for publicity, and photos or videos should only be shared when appropriate and consented to.

Clear communication ↗

We explain the proposed activity, costs, coordination/service fee and limitations before confirming the plan.

Thoughtful execution ↗

We plan for practical needs, safe handling, appropriate sourcing and responsible coordination—not just the appearance of a gesture.

Honest updates ↗

We share completion information and available documentation honestly. We don't promise receipts, tax benefits or impact metrics that don't apply.

What IRA SOUL is—and isn't

IRA SOUL is an occasion-based kindness coordination concept. Unless separately stated and verified, it should not be represented as a registered charitable organization or imply that contributions are tax-deductible donations. Customers engage us for an agreed activity and service, with transparent pricing and scope.
