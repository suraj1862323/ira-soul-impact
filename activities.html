Choose an activity that feels right to you. We'll discuss quantities, sourcing, feasibility, budget, logistics and what completion update can be shared before confirming.

Request an activity ↗

01 · Food & meal distribution ↗

Arrange freshly prepared meals, packed food or refreshments for people who could use a meal. We can discuss meal count, menu, safe handling, distribution approach and location based on the budget and available arrangements.

Consider: meal count · menu · delivery · timing

02 · School stationery & learning kits ↗

Put useful learning materials into students' hands—such as notebooks, pencils, pens, erasers, geometry basics or age-appropriate supplies. Kit contents can be planned around a school or community need.

Consider: kit contents · age/grade · quantity

03 · Animal feeding & care ↗

Support community animals with suitable food or basic care supplies. Plans should consider species-appropriate feeding, local conditions, safe practices and coordination with experienced caregivers when needed.

Consider: species · food type · caregiver coordination

04 · Grocery & essentials kits ↗

Prepare practical household kits with staples or everyday necessities. Contents and quantity can be adapted to the budget and the intended recipients' needs, with dignity and privacy in mind.

Consider: staples · household size · packing

05 · Clothes, blankets & seasonal warmth ↗

Arrange clean, useful clothing, blankets or seasonal essentials. We can plan sizes, quality, weather suitability and whether new or gently used items are appropriate for the activity.

Consider: season · sizes · item condition

06 · Plantation & environment ↗

Make a greener contribution through saplings or community environmental activities. Planting is only one part; location permission, suitable species, watering and ongoing care are important for success.

Consider: species · site · permissions · aftercare
✳

07 · Your own idea ↗

Have something specific in mind? Propose a small celebration, community need, creative act of care or activity that isn't listed. We'll review it with you and confirm if we can coordinate it responsibly.

Consider: your idea · feasibility · timeline

Activity costs are explained before confirmation.

Your total plan may include direct activity costs, supplies, logistics and a clearly communicated IRA SOUL coordination/service fee. We will confirm the plan with you before proceeding.
