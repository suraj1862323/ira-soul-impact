A SIMPLE, PERSONAL PROCESS

Kindness, from idea
to action.

You tell us what you're celebrating, what kind of impact you'd like to make, and your budget. We coordinate the activity and keep you informed along the way.

Start your request ↗
FOUR STEPS

Here's how your idea
comes to life.

01

Share the occasion ↗

Tell us the reason behind your kindness—birthday, anniversary, wedding, achievement, festival, remembrance, or simply a day you want to share. The story is optional; your plan can be as personal or as simple as you like.

02

Choose the activity & budget ↗

Pick a kindness activity from our options, choose an approximate budget, and share preferences such as location, dietary considerations, item types, or timing. If you have a different idea, describe it in the custom request.

03

We confirm the plan ↗

We contact you to discuss feasibility, the proposed activity, budget breakdown, logistics and any coordination/service charges. Nothing should proceed until the plan and costs are agreed with you.

04

Activity & update ↗

Once agreed, we coordinate execution. Afterwards, we provide a completion update and available evidence—such as a summary, receipts where available, or photos/videos where appropriate and consented to.

BUDGET CLARITY

Know what your budget
is meant to cover.

Before confirming, we'll explain the planned activity spend, supplies, delivery or transport costs, and IRA SOUL's coordination/service fee. Actual costs may vary based on the activity, location, quantities and timing. We will communicate changes and seek your approval rather than silently changing the plan.

Activity budget

The items, food, supplies or direct resources intended for the chosen activity.

Logistics

Transport, delivery, sourcing and practical coordination costs, where applicable.

Service & coordination

IRA SOUL's fee for planning, arranging, communicating and completing the activity.
